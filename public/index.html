How this works

Each word is embedded with Google's gemini-embedding-2 and projected onto an axis anchored on the words “bouba” and “kiki”, sharpened by 130 round, soft things and 130 sharp, spiky ones.

The percentage is a classifier's probability: it sorts held-out words correctly 97% of the time. It scores what the model associates with a word, which is not the same as how it sounds.
